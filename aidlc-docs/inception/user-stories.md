# User stories

1. As a learner, I paste the first lesson link of a deeplearning.ai course, click **Download Full Course**, see all modules and lessons, and get every video as `Course/MM - Module/NN - Lesson.mp4` with subtitles.
2. As a learner, when a lesson is locked, I see "Login to unlock", log in once in the app, and the download retries by itself.
3. As a viewer, I paste a YouTube video or playlist link and click **Download Single** / **Download Playlist**.
4. As a user of another course site, I log in via **Sites & logins → Log in to another site**, paste a lesson URL, and the crawler finds the other lessons (sidebar or "Next" links) and downloads their streams.
5. As a user, I can close the app mid-download and continue later without re-downloading finished lessons.
6. As a user, DRM-protected lessons are clearly marked "skipped" instead of failing silently.
